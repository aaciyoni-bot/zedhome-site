Frequently Asked Questions

Are the products genuine and new?

Yes. Every item we ship is brand new and sealed, sourced from top-rated international suppliers — the same warehouses that supply shops and homes across Africa.

Do I need a credit card or bank account?

No! That's the whole point of Kayachi. All you need is a Mobile Money wallet — MTN MoMo, Airtel Money or Zamtel Kwacha — with enough balance for your order.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive within 12–25 days, depending on the item and your location in Zambia. We email you tracking updates along the way.

Will electrical appliances work in Zambia?

Yes. We supply appliances rated for 220–240V, the standard in Zambia. Where a plug differs, a simple adapter sorts it out — and every listing shows the specs so you know exactly what you're getting before you pay.

Is the price I see the final price?

The price shown includes our service and delivery handling. At checkout a small service fee (5%) is added to cover mobile money charges — the total you see at checkout is exactly what you pay.

What if my order arrives faulty or never comes?

You're covered by our money-back guarantee: if your order doesn't arrive, or arrives faulty, we refund you in full to your mobile money wallet. Just email us at office@orizisgroup.com with your order number.

How do I talk to a real person?

Tap the green WhatsApp button in the Support section below — our team replies 7 days a week, 08:00–20:00. Keep your order number handy so we can help faster.

Need help choosing?

Orders, tracking, refunds or just finding the right appliance for your kitchen — email our team at office@orizisgroup.com, 7 days a week, 08:00–20:00.
